# Makoto v0.2 and v0.3 failure-mode coverage

**Status:** Normative companion to Section 8 of the [v0.2](v0.2.md#8-guarantee-model) and [v0.3](v0.3.md#8-guarantee-model) specifications

**Evidence review:** Public pages and the supplied public-talk record reviewed on September 16, 2026

This matrix keeps producer controls, receiver results, and residual risk separate. A track level says how origin or transformation evidence was produced. A verified property says what a verifier established for a particular handoff. A diagnostic says exactly why an attempted verification failed. None of them is a correctness score.

## Coverage matrix

The “level/property” column names the first useful producer level and the receiver property when one applies. `Base` means the ordinary Makoto verification report handles the threat without inventing a property.

The summary evaluator has a separate closed diagnostic vocabulary because these are summary outcomes, not new bundle-verification failures: `E_ORIGIN_LEVEL_1` through `E_ORIGIN_LEVEL_3`, `E_TRANSFORM_LEVEL_1` through `E_TRANSFORM_LEVEL_3`, `E_ASSESSMENT_TRUST`, `E_ASSESSMENT_SIGNATURE`, `E_ORIGIN_ASSESSMENT_SUBJECTS`, `E_ORIGIN_ASSESSMENT_INPUTS`, `E_TRANSFORM_ASSESSMENT_SUBJECTS`, `E_TRANSFORM_ASSESSMENT_INPUTS`, `E_PROPERTY_AUTHORIZED`, `E_PROPERTY_GRAPH_COMPLETE`, `E_PROPERTY_FRESHNESS_ANCHORED`, `E_PROPERTY_SCHEMA_CONFORMANT`, `E_PROPERTY_REPRODUCED`, `E_REQUIRED_VERIFICATION_RESULT`, and `E_REPORT_DENIED`. These codes appear in the deterministic evaluation result and never replace the more specific bundle diagnostics below. The signed VSA itself keeps the standard SLSA v1 predicate shape and communicates `PASSED` or `FAILED` plus the established levels and properties.

| Failure mode | Level / property | Deterministic verifier check and diagnostics | Residual risk or boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D1 source impersonation / false origin claim** — fake sensor data, forged API response, spoofed device, or misleading source URI. | Origin L1 supplies the attributable claim; Origin L2 authenticates the capture platform; `MAKOTO_AUTHORIZED` evaluates receiver authority. | Verify the origin DSSE and source binding; require the platform VSA when claiming L2; require report `authorization` pass. Denials: `E_SIGNATURE_INVALID`, `E_SIGNER_UNAUTHORIZED`, `E_ARTIFACT_DIGEST`. | An authorized collector or platform can still lie, and Makoto cannot establish physical truth. |
| **D2 data tampering / artifact mutation / poisoned pipeline** — bytes change in transit or at rest after sign-off. | Origin L1 or Transform L1 plus Base; `MAKOTO_GRAPH_COMPLETE` supports descendant impact analysis. | Hash received and supplied historical bytes; compare exact signed subject and dataset-entry digests and sizes. Denials: `E_ARTIFACT_DIGEST`, `E_ARTIFACT_SIZE`, `E_ARTIFACT_MISSING`, `E_INPUT_DIGEST`. | A producer can sign bytes that were already bad. Unavailable historical bytes remain `not_checked`. |
| **D3 lineage falsification / graph rewiring / fabricated or cyclic chain**. | Transform L1; `MAKOTO_GRAPH_COMPLETE`. | Recompute statement digests, predecessor statement/subject/input bindings, graph roots and heads, and signed manifest sets. Denials: `E_STATEMENT_DIGEST`, `E_PREDECESSOR_MISSING`, `E_PREDECESSOR_SUBJECT`, `E_INPUT_DIGEST`, `E_GRAPH_CYCLE`, `E_ROOT_INVALID`, `E_MANIFEST_SET`. | A compromised authorized producer can sign a different internally consistent graph; Transform L2/L3 reduce but cannot eliminate that risk. |
| **Branch omission** — an inconvenient but valid branch is left out of the presented history. | Transform L1; `MAKOTO_GRAPH_COMPLETE`. | Require an authorized handoff and exact equality among manifest statements, reachable graph, roots, heads, and final artifacts. Denial: `E_MANIFEST_SET`; selection mismatches also use `E_EXPECTED_HEAD` or `E_EXPECTED_MANIFEST`. | The producer-declared set can omit an event the receiver never independently expected. |
| **D4 timestamp manipulation, replay, rollback, staleness, 800-day records, or a three-hour expiry SLO**. | Base; `MAKOTO_FRESHNESS_ANCHORED`. | Treat `occurredAt` and `issuedAt` as claims; evaluate expected manifest/head/artifact, nonce, or `maxAgeSeconds` at consumer-controlled time. Denials: `E_EXPECTED_MANIFEST`, `E_EXPECTED_HEAD`, `E_EXPECTED_ARTIFACT`, `E_HANDOFF_NONCE`, `E_HANDOFF_STALE`, `E_FRESHNESS_REQUIRED`. | No signed timestamp proves a trusted clock. Rollback resistance is only as strong as the independent anchor channel. |
| **D5 unauthorized attribution / signer abuse** — known or stolen key outside its role, unauthorized author, test key in production, or a keyless certificate for the wrong workflow. | Origin L2 or Transform L2 identifies the platform; `MAKOTO_AUTHORIZED` decides authority. | Separate cryptographic validity from receiver rules for source, operation, profile, and handoff thresholds. Keyless verification requires exact issuer, subject, repository, workflow-ref, and event claims plus a receiver-pinned trust root and valid Rekor evidence. Denial: `E_SIGNER_UNAUTHORIZED` or `E_SIGNATURE_INVALID`. | A stolen key that remains authorized or a compromised allowed workflow can pass. Identity lifecycle and public revocation remain external. |
| **Missing or null approval** — the author is unauthorized or an approver is absent. | Origin L3 or Transform L2; `MAKOTO_AUTHORIZED` and `MAKOTO_SCHEMA_CONFORMANT`. | Authorization rules check author/platform keys; a digest-pinned required profile rejects a missing or null approver. Denials: `E_SIGNER_UNAUTHORIZED`, `E_PROFILE_INVALID`, `E_REQUIRED_PROFILE_MISSING`. | Makoto verifies that approval evidence satisfies policy, not that the approver made a good decision. |
| **D6 attestation forgery / metadata mutation / config-history edit** — signed payload, Sigstore log material, or index is edited or substituted. | Origin L2 or Transform L2; Base. | Strictly parse JSON, recompute decoded payload digest, verify DSSE over exact bytes, and check Sigstore certificate, inclusion proof, checkpoint, and log-entry consistency before graph use. Denials: `E_SIGNATURE_INVALID`, `E_STATEMENT_DIGEST`, `E_ENVELOPE_MALFORMED`, `E_PAYLOAD_TYPE`, `E_JSON_DUPLICATE_KEY`, `E_JSON_INVALID`. | An authorized key holder or allowed workload identity can create a new valid false attestation. |
| **Profile substitution** — a familiar schema URI resolves to weaker bytes or omits transitive references. | Base; `MAKOTO_SCHEMA_CONFORMANT`. | Resolve from authenticated local catalog; compare root and closure digests; require exact consumer profile. Denials: `E_PROFILE_UNRESOLVED`, `E_PROFILE_INVALID`, `E_REQUIRED_PROFILE_MISSING`, `E_CATALOG_INVALID`. | Consumer policy must require the intended profile and protect its catalog. |
| **Missing, malformed, or incomplete licence declaration** — a producer omits the claim, uses invalid SPDX syntax, or leaves a subject uncovered. | v0.3 Base; `MAKOTO_AUTHORIZED` through a rule's profile constraint. | Require the exact standard licence profile and validate subject coverage, SPDX grammar, and evidence URLs. An absent profile cannot match the rule and results in `E_SIGNER_UNAUTHORIZED`; a false attached claim uses `E_PROFILE_INVALID`. | The profile records who vouched for a declaration. It does not establish legal rights, factual accuracy, or evidence sufficiency. |
| **D7 privacy violation** — metadata oversharing or direct identifiers reintroduced into a public/training artifact. | Origin L3 may enforce collection minimization; `MAKOTO_SCHEMA_CONFORMANT`. | Require a digest-pinned artifact profile and validate supported structured content. Denials: `E_PROFILE_INVALID`, `E_REQUIRED_PROFILE_MISSING`, `E_ARTIFACT_FORMAT`. | Provenance is not encryption. Metadata minimization, retention, legal basis, and report handling remain producer/consumer duties. |
| **D8 stream injection** — spoofed events, altered order, or polluted windows. | No live-stream level; Transform L1 applies only after a finite partition is committed. Base verifies committed partitions. | Validate the exact sorted dataset-manifest membership and every supplied partition digest/size. Denials: `E_DATASET_MANIFEST_INVALID`, `E_DATASET_MANIFEST_REQUIRED`, `E_ARTIFACT_DIGEST`, `E_ARTIFACT_SIZE`, `E_RESOURCE_LIMIT`. | Neither version defines live streaming or window semantics, and neither can prove an object-store prefix has no extra objects. |
| **Record substitution inside a verified dataset entry** — a receiver needs evidence for one producer-declared record without transferring or parsing the full logical dataset. | v0.3 record-proof operation after Base bundle verification. | Require an allowed v0.3 bundle, bind the proof to the signed manifest subject and entry digest, and verify the domain-separated Merkle audit path. Optional record bytes must match `recordDigest`. | Makoto proves inclusion of a producer-declared digest. It does not establish that a byte range or hash corresponds to a Parquet or CSV row, and it does not parse the format. |
| **D9 display-layer obfuscation / invisible Unicode** — bytes hidden from human review were present before signing. | Origin L3 can enforce a render-safety control; `MAKOTO_SCHEMA_CONFORMANT` and `MAKOTO_AUTHORIZED`. | Require the exact render-safety profile in the origin rule and validate its signed scan result. Denials: `E_PROFILE_INVALID`, `E_REQUIRED_PROFILE_MISSING`, or `E_SIGNER_UNAUTHORIZED` when no constrained rule authorizes it. | The guarantee is limited by the scanner and profile; unsupported formats require adapters. |
| **Resource exhaustion** — hostile bundle, graph, JSON, schema closure, regex, or report size. | Base, independent of track. | Enforce bootstrap and policy limits before expensive work and isolate profile evaluation. Denial: `E_RESOURCE_LIMIT`; bounded optional work may warn with `W_PROFILE_RESOURCE_LIMIT` or `W_ARTIFACT_VALIDATION_LIMIT`. | Limits are implementation security controls, not proof that all denial-of-service is impossible. |
| **Content hazard / poisoned label / biased or wrong data** present before signing, including SQL-like payloads, NaN values, or physically impossible readings. | Origin L3 can require declared scans; `MAKOTO_SCHEMA_CONFORMANT` checks only expressible policy. | Validate required content profiles and bind downstream evaluation attestations to exact artifact digests. Denials for declared rules: `E_PROFILE_INVALID` or `E_REQUIRED_PROFILE_MISSING`. | **Content correctness, quality, safety, bias, and malicious-but-authorized inputs are out of scope.** Passing integrity and schema checks is not a correctness claim. |
| **Scientific reproducibility gap** — raw version, outlier rule, tool, parameters, or intermediate evidence is missing. | Transform L1 preserves the method trace; `MAKOTO_GRAPH_COMPLETE`; `MAKOTO_REPRODUCED` only after an independent repeat. | Require exact roots, tool and parameter digests, intermediate bindings, and complete handoff; compare the complete sorted transformation-node tuple multiset and final subjects across two independent Transform L2+ reports. Missing graph evidence uses `E_PREDECESSOR_MISSING`, `E_INPUT_DIGEST`, `E_ARTIFACT_MISSING`, or `E_MANIFEST_SET`. When individually valid runs do not match or reuse an independence group, the VSA summary emits `E_PROPERTY_REPRODUCED` and MUST NOT issue the property. | Makoto cannot reconstruct evidence that was never captured or prove instrument calibration. Reproduction can faithfully repeat a wrong method. |
| **CI/GitHub Action or dataset release treated as proof** — a green job or valid signature is mistaken for authorized provenance. | Transform L2 requires the hosted platform itself to generate/sign provenance; `MAKOTO_AUTHORIZED`. | Verify the platform VSA, exact assessment-policy digest, and receiver authorization instead of a badge or workflow name. Denial: `E_SIGNER_UNAUTHORIZED`; changed bytes use `E_ARTIFACT_DIGEST`. | CI is a trigger, not automatically the attester; compromised platform authority remains a trust risk. |
| **Configuration postmortem gap** — who/what/when exists, but exact configuration or affected outputs are not bound. | Transform L1; `MAKOTO_GRAPH_COMPLETE`. | Treat configuration as an exact input artifact and bind its digest, predecessor, operation, and outputs. Missing or changed bindings use `E_INPUT_DIGEST`, `E_PREDECESSOR_MISSING`, `E_ARTIFACT_MISSING`, or `E_SIGNATURE_INVALID`. | Human intent, wisdom of the change, alerting, and root-cause judgment remain external. |
| **Schema drift / missing source ID, ingestion timestamp, or run ID**. | Origin L3 or Transform L1; `MAKOTO_SCHEMA_CONFORMANT`. | Require digest-pinned predicate or artifact profiles that make the use-case fields mandatory; report every required profile as resolved and passing. Denials: `E_PROFILE_INVALID`, `E_REQUIRED_PROFILE_MISSING`. | A syntactically present ID or timestamp can still be false. These fields remain use-case profiles, not universal core requirements. |
| **Wrong chatbot answer: 365-day policy versus the real 30-day policy**. | Origin/Transform tracks preserve provenance; `MAKOTO_FRESHNESS_ANCHORED`, `MAKOTO_AUTHORIZED`, and `MAKOTO_GRAPH_COMPLETE` identify stale or untrusted inputs. | Trace answer evidence to the exact policy record and inference/post-training graph; apply the freshness SLO and authority rules. Missing lineage uses `E_PREDECESSOR_MISSING`, `E_INPUT_DIGEST`, or `E_MANIFEST_SET`; stale or unexpected evidence uses `E_HANDOFF_STALE`, `E_EXPECTED_MANIFEST`, `E_EXPECTED_HEAD`, or `E_EXPECTED_ARTIFACT`; unauthorized evidence uses `E_SIGNER_UNAUTHORIZED`. | Makoto cannot decide which policy text is substantively correct or whether the model interpreted it correctly. A downstream correctness evaluator must make that judgment. |
| **Inference or post-training lineage breaks away from the training anchor**. | Transform L1; `MAKOTO_GRAPH_COMPLETE`. | Append new transform statements that bind the training/model anchor hash, post-training inputs, prompts or configuration digests where available, and outputs; never rewrite the training graph. Missing links use `E_PREDECESSOR_MISSING`, `E_PREDECESSOR_SUBJECT`, `E_INPUT_DIGEST`, or `E_MANIFEST_SET`. | Runtime nondeterminism and model semantic behavior require separate evidence; sensitive prompts may need privacy-preserving references. |
| **Copies lose blast-radius evidence** across object storage, warehouse marts, feature store, backup, vendor SaaS, or an old notebook. | Transform L1; `MAKOTO_GRAPH_COMPLETE`. | Each participating copy or transformation appends a statement with predecessor and output digests; traverse descendants from the bad root. Gaps in the presented graph use `E_PREDECESSOR_MISSING`, `E_INPUT_DIGEST`, or `E_MANIFEST_SET`. A copy never presented to Makoto has no diagnostic. | Copies made outside the evidence graph are invisible. Makoto is not a universal discovery system. |
| **Deletion or retention policy is lost downstream**. | Origin L3 may require a retention/SLO profile; `MAKOTO_SCHEMA_CONFORMANT` carries it through declared handoffs. | Require the profile on matching statements/artifacts and verify every participating handoff. Denials: `E_REQUIRED_PROFILE_MISSING`, `E_PROFILE_INVALID`. | Makoto records obligations and custody; it cannot delete unknown copies or prove a storage system honored deletion. |
| **The exact bytes that trained a model are unknown**. | Origin L1 plus Transform L1; `MAKOTO_GRAPH_COMPLETE`. | Bind source, preprocessing, dataset manifest, model-training input, and model artifact digests through the graph and exact handoff sets. Denials: `E_ARTIFACT_DIGEST`, `E_ARTIFACT_MISSING`, `E_INPUT_DIGEST`, `E_PREDECESSOR_MISSING`, or `E_MANIFEST_SET`. | A digest identifies bytes, not whether the data was representative, licensed, or correct. |
| **Custody or downstream policy becomes ambiguous** — who made, approved, or may use each copy is unclear. | Origin L3 for capture controls and Transform L2 for platform identity; `MAKOTO_AUTHORIZED` and `MAKOTO_SCHEMA_CONFORMANT`. | Verify signer roles and required approval/retention profiles under consumer policy at every handoff. Denials: `E_SIGNER_UNAUTHORIZED`, `E_PROFILE_INVALID`, `E_REQUIRED_PROFILE_MISSING`. | Organizational responsibility and legal accountability are broader than cryptographic identity. |
| **Normalization, filtering, enrichment, redaction, or newline conversion silently changes the dataset**. | Transform L1; `MAKOTO_GRAPH_COMPLETE`; Base artifact checks. | Every meaningful byte change creates a new subject and transform statement; compare exact input/output digests and graph edges. Denials: `E_ARTIFACT_DIGEST`, `E_INPUT_DIGEST`, `E_MANIFEST_SET`. | Makoto shows that bytes changed and under which claim; it does not decide whether the change preserved intended meaning. |

## Correctness handoff

Makoto will never certify data-content correctness. A downstream process can consume Makoto's exact origin, operation, signer, graph, profile, and artifact identities; run domain tests, human review, calibration checks, retrieval evaluation, or model evaluation; and bind that result to the same digests in a separate attestation. That division keeps Makoto simple and transparent while making incorrect outcomes diagnosable and replaceable.
